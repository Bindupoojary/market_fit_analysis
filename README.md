      <h2>Detailed Breakdown of Dashboard Views</h2>

      <h3>AQI Severity & Environmental Risk View</h3>
      <p>This view provides a macro-level understanding of India's air quality landscape. It highlights:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Monthly AQI fluctuations with YoY comparison to assess improvement or deterioration.</li>
        <li>State-wise AQI bubble distribution to visually identify geographic pollution clusters.</li>
        <li>Critical days count indicating extreme pollution events requiring urgent intervention.</li>
        <li>Pollutant composition analysis (PM2.5, PM10, SO₂, NO₂, O₃) to trace emission sources.</li>
        <li>Weekday vs weekend AQI variations showing behavioral or industrial impact on pollution.</li>
        <li>EV vehicle penetration compared with AQI to evaluate early transition impact.</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>Health Impact & Respiratory Disease View</h3>
      <p>This module overlays air quality indicators with health outcomes to understand how environmental degradation influences public health. Key insights include:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Disease case trends by month showing seasonal outbreak patterns aligned with pollution peaks.</li>
        <li>Disease-wise breakdown including influenza-like illnesses, H1N1 and other respiratory conditions.</li>
        <li>State-wise respiratory disease distribution identifying medically vulnerable zones.</li>
        <li>AQI vs respiratory case correlation table quantifying exposure–impact relationships.</li>
        <li>Total deaths vs reported cases offering severity understanding for healthcare planning.</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>Executive Market Demand & Opportunity View</h3>
      <p>This view is tailored for business, product, and strategy teams to evaluate the commercial landscape for air purifiers. It consolidates:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Priority score by state combining AQI severity, income, disease load, and market triggers.</li>
        <li>Competitive analysis of major purifier brands on pricing, CADR, features, and coverage.</li>
        <li>Feature gap scoring to identify strengths and weaknesses of competitors.</li>
        <li>State-wise pollutant mapping and recommended filter types based on pollutant dominance.</li>
        <li>Income vs AQI scatter correlation to identify high-demand/high-affordability consumer segments.</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>Overall Project Outcomes</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Holistic Risk Identification: Integrated AQI–health–economic signals revealed 8–12 states requiring urgent policy and business attention.</li>
        <li>Actionable Market Intelligence: Mid-range air purifiers emerged as optimal for most Indian states due to price–feature balance.</li>
        <li>Filter Technology Mapping: Provided pollutant-specific filter recommendations enabling companies to localize product variants.</li>
        <li>Seasonal Intervention Strategy: Identified winter and early monsoon as the most critical windows for both health advisories and purifier sales surges.</li>
        <li>High-Value Decision Framework: Produced a multi-layer system usable by government bodies, healthcare organizations, and consumer brands.</li>
      </ul>
