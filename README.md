Comprehensive AQI, Respiratory Health, and Air Purifier Market Insights for India</h1>

<br>
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes India’s air quality trends, respiratory disease burden, pollution patterns, and market demand indicators to understand:

Which pollutants air purifiers should target

Essential features required in an air purifier

Regions with the highest demand potential

Market size indicators across economic and environmental dimensions

Seasonal and geographic AQI trends

Correlation between pollution and respiratory diseases

Triggers that influence consumer interest in air purifiers

The analysis uses datasets from Dataful, combined with additional research datasets, and is visualized through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

🎯 Project Objectives
1. Severity Mapping

Identify cities and states experiencing persistent, worsening, or seasonal spikes in AQI. Analyze pollutant dominance and long-term patterns.

2. Health Impact Correlation

Examine how pollution relates to respiratory diseases across India. Identify regions with overlapping high AQI and high disease cases, and study seasonal outbreak patterns.

3. Demand Trigger & Market Opportunity Analysis

Analyze how pollution influences consumer behavior. Identify market opportunities using AQI trends, income data, competitor analysis, and pollutant-specific recommendations.

🧠 Key Insights
🔹 Pollutant Analysis

PM2.5 and PM10 are the dominant pollutants across most regions.

Industrial belts show elevated SO2 and NO2 levels.

Certain regions record seasonal O3 spikes.

🔹 High-Severity Regions

Delhi NCR

Uttar Pradesh cluster

Rajasthan

Bihar

Maharashtra

🔹 Health Impact

Disease cases spike during high-pollution months (winter + early monsoon).

Influenza-like illnesses are the most common.

Significant overlap observed between high AQI and high respiratory case states.

🔹 Market Demand Indicators

Strong purifier demand potential in regions with:

High AQI

Moderate to high income

Continuous seasonal spikes

Mid-range purifiers (₹10,000–₹20,000) show strongest competitiveness.

States with severe AQI but low purifier penetration indicate untapped opportunities.
