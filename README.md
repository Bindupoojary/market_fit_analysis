<h2>📌 Project Overview</h2>

<p>This project analyzes India’s air quality trends, respiratory disease burden, pollution patterns, and market demand indicators to understand:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Which pollutants air purifiers should target</li>
  <li>Essential features required in an air purifier</li>
  <li>Regions with the highest demand potential</li>
  <li>Market size indicators across economic and environmental dimensions</li>
  <li>Seasonal and geographic AQI trends</li>
  <li>Correlation between pollution and respiratory diseases</li>
  <li>Triggers that influence consumer interest in air purifiers</li>
</ul>

<p>The analysis uses datasets from <strong>Dataful</strong>, combined with additional research datasets, and is visualized through an interactive <strong>Power BI dashboard</strong>.</p>

<hr style="border: none; border-top: 2px solid #ccc; margin: 25px 0;">

<h2>🎯 Project Objectives</h2>

<h3>1. Severity Mapping</h3>
<p>Identify cities and states experiencing persistent, worsening, or seasonal spikes in AQI. Analyze pollutant dominance and long-term patterns.</p>

<h3>2. Health Impact Correlation</h3>
<p>Examine how pollution relates to respiratory diseases across India. Identify regions with overlapping high AQI and high disease cases, and study seasonal outbreak patterns.</p>

<h3>3. Demand Trigger & Market Opportunity Analysis</h3>
<p>Analyze how pollution influences consumer behavior. Identify market opportunities using AQI trends, income data, competitor analysis, and pollutant-specific recommendations.</p>

<h2>🧠 Key Insights</h2>

<h3>🔹 Pollutant Analysis</h3>
<ul>
  <li>PM2.5 and PM10 are the dominant pollutants across most regions.</li>
  <li>Industrial belts show elevated SO2 and NO2 levels.</li>
  <li>Certain regions record seasonal O3 spikes.</li>
</ul>

<h3>🔹 High-Severity Regions</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Delhi NCR</li>
  <li>Uttar Pradesh cluster</li>
  <li>Rajasthan</li>
  <li>Bihar</li>
  <li>Maharashtra</li>
</ul>

<h3>🔹 Health Impact</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Disease cases spike during high-pollution months (winter + early monsoon).</li>
  <li>Influenza-like illnesses are the most common.</li>
  <li>Significant overlap observed between high AQI and high respiratory case states.</li>
</ul>

<h3>🔹 Market Demand Indicators</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Strong purifier demand potential in regions with high AQI and moderate to high income.</li>
  <li>Continuous seasonal spikes drive sustained demand.</li>
  <li>Mid-range purifiers (₹10,000–₹20,000) show strongest competitiveness.</li>
  <li>States with severe AQI but low purifier penetration indicate untapped opportunities.</li>
</ul>
