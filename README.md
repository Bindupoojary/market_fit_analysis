<h2>📌 Project Overview</h2>
<p>This project analyzes India’s air quality trends, respiratory disease burden, pollutant patterns, and market demand indicators to answer:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Which pollutants air purifiers should target</li>
  <li>Essential features required in an air purifier</li>
  <li>Regions with the highest demand potential</li>
  <li>Market size indicators across economic and environmental dimensions</li>
  <li>Seasonal and geographic AQI trends</li>
  <li>Correlation between pollution and respiratory diseases</li>
  <li>Triggers that influence consumer interest in air purifiers</li>
</ul>

<p>The analysis uses datasets from <strong>Dataful</strong> combined with additional datasets and market research, visualized through an interactive <strong>Power BI</strong> dashboard.</p>

<h2>🎯 Project Objectives</h2>

<h3>1. Severity Mapping</h3>
<p>Identify cities and states experiencing persistent, worsening, or seasonal spikes in AQI. Analyze pollutant dominance and long-term trends to inform interventions.</p>

<h3>2. Health Impact Correlation</h3>
<p>Examine how air quality correlates with respiratory disease incidence, identify high-risk regions, and highlight seasonal outbreak windows aligned with pollution spikes.</p>

<h3>3. Demand Trigger & Market Opportunity Analysis</h3>
<p>Map market opportunity using AQI trends, income brackets, competitor analysis, and pollutant-specific filter recommendations to guide product-market fit and go-to-market timing.</p>

<h2>🧠 Key Insights (Analyst Results & Outcomes)</h2>

<h3>🔹 Pollutant & Spatial Findings</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>PM2.5 and PM10 are dominant</strong> across the majority of urban and peri-urban monitoring locations — primary target for consumer purifiers.</li>
  <li><strong>Industrial clusters</strong> (specific belts in UP, parts of Maharashtra) show elevated SO₂ and NO₂ requiring specialized treatment (activated carbon + chemical scrubbers at scale).</li>
  <li><strong>Seasonal O₃ spikes</strong> observed in particular months in select states, suggesting photochemical activity — useful for product messaging and warnings.</li>
</ul>

<h3>🔹 Health Outcomes</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Respiratory case spikes align with high-AQI months</strong> (winter and early monsoon windows) — validating pollution → health linkage in the data.</li>
  <li><strong>High-risk states</strong> consistently include Delhi NCR, UP cluster, Rajasthan and Bihar — these states show both elevated AQI and higher respiratory case loads.</li>
  <li><strong>Healthcare planning implication:</strong> early alert systems and resource ramp-ups (oxygen, clinics) timed to predicted pollution spikes can reduce short-term morbidity.</li>
</ul>

<h3>🔹 Market & Commercial Outcomes</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Top product-market fit:</strong> mid-range purifiers (~₹10k–₹20k) provide the best balance of CADR, filtration (HEPA + activated carbon), and affordability for most states.</li>
  <li><strong>Regional product recommendations:</strong> PM-dominant states → HEPA-focused units; industrial pollutant states → HEPA + high-adsorption carbon / specialized filters.</li>
  <li><strong>Priority marketing windows:</strong> pre-winter and pre-monsoon campaigns increase conversion by aligning with rising AQI and health concerns.</li>
  <li><strong>Untapped opportunities:</strong> states with very high AQI but low purifier penetration represent immediate sales growth targets with localized pricing & distribution strategies.</li>
</ul>

<h3>🔹 Quantified Outcomes (what the analysis produced)</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Flagged <strong>8–12 high-priority states</strong> for immediate policy and commercial focus using composite scoring.</li>
  <li>Generated <strong>state-level pollutant → filter mappings</strong> enabling SKU localization and inventory planning.</li>
  <li>Delivered competitor feature gap matrix to inform product development priorities (e.g., VOC sensors, app-control, true-HEPA certification).</li>
  <li>Produced seasonal alert schedule and recommended timing for health advisories and marketing promotions.</li>
</ul>
